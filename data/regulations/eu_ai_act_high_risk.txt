EU AI Act high-risk banking model update.

Providers and deployers of high-risk AI systems used for creditworthiness,
pricing, fraud detection, or customer eligibility decisions must establish data
governance controls over training data, validation data, and testing data so
datasets are relevant, representative, accurate, and complete.

The bank must maintain documented lineage and provenance for data used to train
high-risk AI systems, including source systems, transformation history, quality
checks, approvals, and retained evidence for regulatory review.

High-risk AI systems shall automatically record events and logs throughout the
model lifecycle so decisions, model outputs, human interventions, and system
changes remain traceable for audit and supervisory investigation.

The bank must provide effective human oversight for high-risk AI decisions,
including clear escalation paths, authority to override automated outcomes, and
evidence that reviewers understand model limitations.

The bank must ensure model robustness, accuracy, cybersecurity, and ongoing
performance monitoring through validation, backtesting, drift detection, and
documented remediation when model performance falls below approved thresholds.
