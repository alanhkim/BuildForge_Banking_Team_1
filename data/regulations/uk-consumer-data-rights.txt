UK Consumer Data Rights & Open Finance Act (CDROF) — 2026 (synthetic, for Flow 2 testing)
 
Scope: This Act applies to retail banks and payment service providers that share
customer account data with authorised third parties under open finance. It is a
NEW regulation that is not present in the existing catalog, so it is intended to
test the uploaded-regulation (agent) flow.
 
Obligations introduced by this change:
 
1. Firms must obtain explicit, informed consent from the data subject before
   sharing any personal data with a third-party provider, and shall allow the
   consent to be withdrawn at any time.
 
2. Firms shall retain shared customer data for no longer than is necessary for
   the agreed purpose; a documented retention schedule must define when data is
   deleted.
 
3. Firms must apply strong customer authentication before granting a third party
   access to a customer's account information or initiating a payment, using
   multi-factor authentication.
 
4. Firms shall ensure fair value and good customer outcomes when offering open
   finance products, so that data sharing does not lead to poor outcomes for
   vulnerable customers.
 
5. Firms must maintain a data catalog and business glossary describing every
   shared data element and its metadata, so consumers and regulators can
   understand what is shared.
 
6. Firms shall notify affected data subjects and the regulator without undue
   delay following any personal data breach involving shared account data.
